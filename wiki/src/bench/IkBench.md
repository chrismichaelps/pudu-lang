# Ark Ik Benchmark

Times 10,000 two-bone solves toward targets sweeping through, beyond and inside the limb's reach
with soft reach, one at a time and as one [[Ark Limbs]] batch, and 1,000 FABRIK solves of an eight-joint chain toward reachable targets. Prints
nanoseconds per solve for each and panics unless every two-bone solve keeps both bone lengths and
every chain converges.
