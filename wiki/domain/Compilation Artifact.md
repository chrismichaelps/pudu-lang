# Compilation Artifact

- **Definition:** A successful compiler product consisting of generated C11 and, when requested, a native executable plus provenance metadata.
- **Not:** A partially emitted file after diagnostics or toolchain failure.
- **Atomicity:** Outputs are staged and published only after successful generation/toolchain completion.
